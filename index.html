About CTML

    

      
CTML (Chess Tournament Markup Language) represents an entire chess tournament — its participants, standings, and every game — as one validated, self-describing XML file. Where PGN is per-game, CTML is per-tournament, and it captures what a pile of PGNs cannot: who competed, the crosstable that proves the event is complete, teams and pools, and knockout brackets.

      
This page is a client-side viewer, maker, and exporter — everything runs in your browser; nothing is uploaded. The corpus, schema, and builders live in the GitHub repository.

      
    
        
  • Viewer — open any tournament from the corpus or your own .ctml file; browse standings, crosstable, groups, bracket, and replay games on a board.

  • Exporters — download the CTML, a reconstructed PGN, or a JSON rendering of any loaded tournament.

  • Maker — enter an individual event by hand, or paste a PGN to pre-fill it, and generate CTML.
